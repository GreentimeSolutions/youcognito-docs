# YOUCOG art and Grok Voice

Two small AI extras with limited free spots each day.

## Make YOUCOG art (FLUX)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Paused right now.** The art maker is switched off on the live app. The card says so until it is back.
{% endhint %}

Describe an image in a few words (3 to 300 characters) and tap **Make it**. The image is made with Black Forest Labs' FLUX model.

* **First come, first served:** 10 free images a day in total, **1 per person**.
* Resets at **midnight Central time**. The card shows how many spots are left.
* **Save your image soon:** the image link expires in about 10 minutes.
* When the feature is switched off or all spots are taken, the card tells you.

## Listen (Grok Voice)

Every research brief has a **Listen (Grok Voice)** button that reads the answer aloud using xAI's voice.

* Tap **Listen** to play, **Stop** to stop.
* Audio for the same answer is reused in your session, so replaying is instant.
* There's a small daily limit per person and overall. If it's reached, the button says so.
