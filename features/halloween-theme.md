# Halloween theme

For spooky season, Youcognito dresses up. It's purely cosmetic: nothing about how the app works changes.

* Drifting ghosts and purple fog in the background
* Creepster-style spooky headings
* A haunted claim button
* Ghosts and bats that celebrate a successful faucet claim
* Static when you burn a session
* A jack-o'-lantern by the Youcog mark, cobwebs on the cards, candle glow and a glowing moon

If your device is set to **reduce motion**, the animations are toned down.
