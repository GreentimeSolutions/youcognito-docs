# Quote-cast for $RCST

A one-time bonus for Farcaster users: quote-cast the Youcognito launch and get **1,111 $RCST** on Base.

## How it works

1. Open Youcognito **inside Farcaster** (or the Base app).
2. On the **Quote-cast the launch, get 1111 $RCST** card, tap the quote button. A cast opens in the **/recast** channel quoting the [launch cast](https://farcaster.xyz/greentime1/0xa5ad5505), with the app attached.
3. Post it, copy your quote-cast's link, and paste it into the card.
4. Tap **Claim 1,111 RCST**. The server checks that the cast is yours and really quotes the launch, then sends RCST to a wallet **verified on your Farcaster account**.

## Rules

* **Once per Farcaster account, ever.** Also once per wallet and once per cast.
* You need an Ethereum address verified on your Farcaster account.
* There's a daily limit on claims. If it's reached, try again the next day.
* On the web you can **check** a quote-cast link, but claiming needs your Farcaster sign-in.

$RCST contract on Base: `0xcd74cb0455be5bc8316c4c84864988ba9e5631ce`.
