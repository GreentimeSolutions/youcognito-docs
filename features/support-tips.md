# Support Youcog (tips)

Like Youcognito? You can send a small tip on Base. Tips help fund a Google Play listing for Youcognito and keep the faucet topped up.

## How to tip

1. Open the **Support Youcog** card.
2. Pick an amount: **0.0005 ETH**, **0.002 ETH**, **0.004 ETH**, **1 USDC**, **5 USDC** or **10 USDC**.
3. Tap **Tip** and confirm in your wallet. The app switches your wallet to Base if needed.
4. You get a **View on Basescan** link.

Prefer to send it yourself? Copy the donation address from the card. The official donation address is:

`0xa959d8eeded6fb440ca584d1e87e581168bbf628`

## After you tip

Your tip is checked on-chain and then shows on the **Tippers** tab of the [leaderboard](leaderboard-and-stats.md). In Farcaster, it's linked to your Farcaster name.

{% hint style="info" %}
Tips are gifts. They don't buy tokens, perks or any promise of value.
{% endhint %}
