# Web app or Farcaster Mini App

Youcognito is one app with two front doors.

## In your browser

Open [youcognito.vercel.app](https://youcognito.vercel.app) in any browser, on phone or desktop.

* **Faucet:** paste your Base address. One claim per address and per connection (internet connection) each day.
* **Deep Mission:** tap **Verify wallet** and sign a free message in your browser wallet to prove you hold YOUCOG. Signing costs nothing and moves no funds.
* **Buy YOUCOG:** opens the Bankr trade page.

## Inside Farcaster (Mini App)

Find **Youcognito** in Farcaster (or the Base app), or tap a cast that embeds the app and choose **Open Youcognito**.

* **Faucet:** you're signed in with Farcaster automatically (Quick Auth), and your connected wallet is filled in. You can switch to a different address. One claim per Farcaster account per day.
* **Deep Mission:** your Farcaster-verified wallets are checked for you, no signature needed.
* **Buy YOUCOG:** opens the in-app swap, sized for about 10M YOUCOG at the current price.
* **Share:** after a claim you can share your streak to Farcaster.
* **Quote-cast for $RCST:** only claimable inside Farcaster. See [Quote-cast for $RCST](../features/rcst-quote-cast.md).

## Which should I use?

Both work. The Mini App is smoother for claiming (no copy-pasting addresses), and the browser is handy on desktop.
