# Changelog

User-facing changes to Youcognito, newest first. Dates are in US Central time.

Everything listed here shipped to the live app unless it says paused or ended. See [What's live right now](../getting-started/whats-live.md).

## October 21, 2026

* **Docs wording update:** clearer, plainer wording across these docs. Dollar figures were removed, the faucet, listen boost and survey are described as gifts while supplies last, and warning boxes were added to the feature pages. The $RCST quote-cast page is marked ended. Forge and nito sections now say details will be published after review.

## October 20, 2026

* **Ghost claim celebration:** after a confirmed faucet claim, survey gift or boosted claim, Youcog pops up to celebrate and shows the amount received. Tap anywhere or press Esc to close it. With reduce motion on, a still card shows instead.
* **Ghost share images:** links to the app now show a ghost preview card on X and Farcaster.

## October 19, 2026

* **Faucet gift amounts doubled (live):** the daily faucet gift is now **840, 2,222 or 13,938 YOUCOG** (was 420, 1,111 or 6,969), with the same 1.25x and 1.5x streak bonuses on top, up to 20,907 a day. The faucet's daily budget doubled too, to **500,000 YOUCOG a day** (was 250,000). The 100k listen boost and the feedback survey are unchanged. See [Daily faucet](../features/faucet.md).
* **Feedback survey (live):** a 10-question survey inside the app (about 3 minutes). As a thank-you, a one-time YOUCOG gift may be sent from the faucet wallet while supplies last. One per Farcaster account and per wallet, you need one faucet claim first, and only a few gifts go out each day. See [Share feedback](../features/feedback-survey.md).
* **nito DAO governance plan published:** nito voting points (proposed, not live). Details will be published after legal review. nito has no monetary value. See [nito DAO](../token/nito-dao.md).
* **Roadmap updated:** the docs roadmap has four sections: **Now (live)**, **Next**, **Later** and **Maybe**. The feedback survey is under **Now**. See [Roadmap](roadmap.md).
