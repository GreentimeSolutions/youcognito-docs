# Roadmap

What's live and what's coming. Items under **Next**, **Later** and **Maybe** are planned, not promises, and have no fixed dates. For a full history of what shipped, see the [Changelog](changelog.md).

## Now (live)

* **Private research** with sealed sessions you can burn when you're done
* **Deep Mission** for holders who want a longer, sourced report
* **Daily YOUCOG faucet** with streaks
* **Listen boost** through the [Youcognito Theme (Remix) on SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/greentime1/youcognito-theme-remix)

## Next (the next few weeks)

* **Forge MVP:** three tutorial quests (first claim, first swap, approve and forge), free soulbound badges, and cheap Streak Shields and Deep Mission Passes, tested on testnet first
* **Feedback form** with a YOUCOG reward for people who fill it out
* **Token info** on Basescan and in the Base app / Coinbase Wallet

## Later (through the nito DAO)

* **$NITO** governance voting (in design, not launched, no promised value). See [nito DAO and $NITO](../token/nito-dao.md)
* **YOUCOG staking** for rewards or perks
* **Forge boosters and studio packs**
* **POAPs** for finishing quests
* **Ghost pfp cosmetics** (hats, glasses, outfits and other accessories)
* **Holder bundles**
* **AI agent payments** through an x402 gateway
* **Original Youcognito Theme on SoundCloud** (the Remix is already out)
* **Custom domain** for the app

## Maybe

* **Gas sponsoring** (CDP Paymaster) if gas costs from daily users get expensive
* **Card onramp** so people can buy with a card
