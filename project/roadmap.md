# Roadmap

What is live and what is planned. Items under **Next**, **Later** and **Maybe** are planned, no dates. They are not promises, may change or never happen, and proposed items are pending review. For a full history of what shipped, see the [Changelog](changelog.md).

## Now (live)

* **Private research** with sealed sessions you can burn when you are done
* **Deep Mission** for holders who want a longer, sourced report
* **Daily YOUCOG faucet** with streaks (a small gift while supplies last)
* **Listen boost:** finish the [Youcognito Theme (Remix)](https://soundcloud.com/greentime1/youcognito-theme-remix) to unlock a bigger faucet gift that day, while supplies last
* **Feedback survey:** 10 quick questions for faucet claimers, with a one-time YOUCOG thank-you gift while supplies last. See [Share feedback](../features/feedback-survey.md)
* **Ghost claim celebration** and ghost share images

## Next (planned, no dates)

* **Forge (in design):** optional learning quests and cosmetic badges. Details will be published after review. Streak Shields will not be sold.
* **Token metadata** (logo and description) on Basescan.

## Later (planned, no dates, pending review)

* **nito DAO** (proposed, not live, lawyer review pending): nito voting points (proposed, not live). Details will be published after legal review. See [nito DAO](../token/nito-dao.md)
* **POAPs** for finishing quests
* **Ghost pfp cosmetics** (hats, glasses, outfits and other accessories)
* **AI agent payments** through an x402 gateway
* **Original Youcognito Theme on SoundCloud** (the Remix is already out)
* **Custom domain** for the app

## Maybe (pending review)

* **Gas sponsoring** (CDP Paymaster) if gas costs from daily users get expensive
* **Card onramp**
