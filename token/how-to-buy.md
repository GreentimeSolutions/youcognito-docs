# How to get YOUCOG

## Free: the daily faucet

Claim 420, 1,111 or 6,969 YOUCOG every day at the [faucet](../features/faucet.md). Build a streak for up to 1.5x, or [listen to the remix](../features/listen-boost.md) to boost a claim to 100,000.

## Buy on Bankr

* **Bankr terminal:** [bankr.bot/terminal?refCode=BGQMYWJW-BNKR](https://bankr.bot/terminal?refCode=BGQMYWJW-BNKR)
* Always check the contract: `0x92Bff5D88157114DfC4a0A00F3496AA7e2E7EbA3` on Base.

## Buy inside the app

* **Buy YOUCOG** on the faucet card, and **Get 10M YOUCOG (about $1)** on the Deep Mission card.
* **In Farcaster:** opens the in-app swap from ETH on Base, sized for about 10M YOUCOG at the live GeckoTerminal price (plus a small buffer). You confirm in your wallet.
* **On the web:** opens Bankr's YOUCOG trade page.

You'll need a little **ETH on Base** to swap and pay network fees.

## New to crypto?

Tap **New to crypto? Get Coinbase Wallet** next to the Buy buttons to set up a wallet that works on Base.
