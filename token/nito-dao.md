# nito DAO and $NITO (planned)

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Planned, in design, not launched.** The nito DAO and $NITO don't exist yet. There is no $NITO token, no contract, no sale and no airdrop. Nothing here promises any value. Anyone offering you $NITO today is not us.
{% endhint %}

## The idea

Youcognito plans a two-token setup:

| Token | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **$YOUCOG** | Live today. The **trading and staking** token: faucet, holder perks, and planned staking. |
| **$NITO** (planned) | The **governance** token: **voting power** in the nito DAO. |

The DAO's name is written in lowercase: **nito DAO**.

## What the nito DAO would do

The plan is for the nito DAO to:

* **Run the backend decisions together:** how the faucet budget, boosts and limits are set, what gets built next, and how community funds are used.
* **Vote with $NITO:** holders propose and vote on changes.
* **Plan marketing for $NITO and Youcognito:** campaigns, partnerships and community programs, decided in the open.

## What's still being designed

Everything, including:

* How $NITO would be distributed, and how much exists
* How voting works (thresholds, quorum, what's binding)
* How YOUCOG staking relates to $NITO, if at all
* Which tools the DAO would use

When there's something real to share, it will be announced on the [official channels](../getting-started/official-links.md) and added to the [Changelog](../project/changelog.md).
