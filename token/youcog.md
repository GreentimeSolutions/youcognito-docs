# $YOUCOG

**$YOUCOG** is Youcognito's community token on **Base**. It's what the daily faucet gives out, what unlocks [Deep Mission](../features/deep-mission.md), and the token for trading (and, later, staking).

{% hint style="warning" %}
YOUCOG has **no promised value**. It's a community token for an experimental app. Read the [Disclaimer](../project/disclaimer.md).
{% endhint %}

## What YOUCOG is used for today

* **Daily faucet:** 420, 1,111 or 6,969 a day, with streak bonuses. See [Daily faucet](../features/faucet.md).
* **Listen boost:** a full listen of the remix boosts a claim to 100,000. See [Listen boost](../features/listen-boost.md).
* **Deep Mission:** hold 10,000,000 YOUCOG (about $1) to unlock longer reports.
* **Holder badge** on the leaderboard.

## Planned

* **Staking**, **boosters and multipliers**, and **tasks**. See the [Roadmap](../project/roadmap.md).
* YOUCOG stays the **trading and staking** token. Voting would be handled by a separate, planned governance token, **$NITO**. See [nito DAO and $NITO](nito-dao.md).
